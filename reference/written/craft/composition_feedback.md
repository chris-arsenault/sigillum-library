# Composition feedback: coverage and reuse

This is the entry point for lessons from composition feedback. The linked craft
references own the guidance; this register records coverage, provenance and
where a future composition should apply it. It is not a new score contract or
a collection of universal musical rules.

## How feedback enters the library

Read the user's actual correction and the material it concerns. Record the
failed relationship, the useful alternative and its scope. Distinguish an
active boundary (such as an approved ending) from a directional correction
(such as needing more contrast). Preserve exceptions and successful material.
Update the owning reference and any conflicting recipe at the same time.

Use an existing executable card when its notes demonstrate the relationship;
add a new specimen only for a distinct missing musical mechanism, following
the card-writing procedure. A usage note must say what the specimen actually
does and what must be adapted. Card names and projection counts do not prove
the lesson has been realized in a new piece.

Route the lesson into normal composition, revision and completion review.
When the user is hand-correcting or asks only to record feedback, update the
library without changing the score or overwriting exports. Do not create a
new standalone feedback document for each remark; extend the relevant owner
and this coverage record when it adds a distinct lesson.

## Executable examples already available

These source files were read when mapping the feedback; their musical notes
are unchanged by this guidance update.

- [D9_REINFORCE_CONTRAST](../../../technique_library/dsl/cards/dialogue/d9_reinforce_contrast.rb)
  alternates lead reinforcement with accompaniment answers. Adapt its relationships
  to actual phrase gaps; do not reproduce its bar allocation as a rule.
- [X2_BROKEN_CALL](../../../technique_library/dsl/cards/callresponse/x2_broken_call.rb)
  develops interruption through changing initiative and a role reversal. Its
  phrase-level argument offers a miniature story; it is not itself a universal
  paragraph-boundary prescription.
- [OA2_CONCERTATO](../../../technique_library/dsl/cards/orch.antiphony/oa2_concertato.rb)
  alternates a nimble small group with stronger ensemble statements, tightens
  the exchanges and combines their roles at the cadence. Adapt the changing
  relationship to the actual forces rather than copying an orchestral roster.
